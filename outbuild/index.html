How to play

Jump out of the airship, land, grab weapons from chests and the floor, and be the last one standing. The storm shrinks the island in phases — stay inside the white circle.

W A S D Move
Mouse Look · LMB Shoot / build · RMB Aim
Space Jump · leave the airship · open the glider
Shift Sprint · Ctrl Crouch
1 Harvesting tool · 2–6 Items · Wheel Cycle
E Pick up / open · G Drop · R Reload
Q Build mode · Z Wall · X Floor · C Ramp · V Roof
R in build mode: change material (wood · stone · metal)
F Edit your own build: add a window or door, turn a ramp
M Map · B Dance · Esc Pause

Hit trees, rocks, cars and houses with the harvesting tool to collect wood, stone and metal. Hitting the glowing blue spot gives a bonus. Each piece costs 10 materials. Wood builds fastest, metal is strongest.
