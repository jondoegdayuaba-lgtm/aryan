How to play

WASD move
Mouse look, Left fire, Right scope / stab / short throw
Shift walk quietly
Ctrl or C crouch
Space jump (crouch in the air to crouch-jump)
R reload
1–5 or wheel switch weapon
Q last weapon
G drop weapon
E pick up / defuse / plant
B buy menu
Tab scoreboard
Esc pause
Plant: hold 5 (bomb) + left click, or hold E on site.
Defuse: hold E at the bomb (5 s with a kit).
Pull the mouse down while spraying to control recoil. Standing still makes the first shot accurate.
Coins: every finished match pays out for rounds won, kills, assists, MVPs and the result (more on Hard).

Ctrl+W closes browser tabs, so the game runs fullscreen with the keyboard locked when your browser allows it; C also crouches. Names, maps and art are original to this project.
