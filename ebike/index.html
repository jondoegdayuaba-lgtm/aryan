Touch: hold ◀ ▶ to steer. The bike accelerates by itself. Gamepad: stick, triggers, A boost, X drift, Y look back.

  • Heat ★ starts at 2. Every star brings more and faster cruisers. From 3 stars, police e-bike riders join in, and some cars come at you head-on.
  • Shake them: stay more than 150 m from every cruiser for 8 seconds to lose a star. Lose them all to escape.
  • Busted when cruisers stay close and the meter fills, faster if you’re slow. A wrecked bike ends the run too.
  • Score from distance, close calls with traffic, batteries and wrecked cruisers.
  • Real e-bike chases are won in tight spaces: cut through the alleys between buildings and ride the sidewalks.
  • Parked cars, market stalls and scaffolding are solid. Weave, don’t plough.
  • Cops slow down for corners. Drift round them and pull away.
  • Ram a cruiser at speed to wreck it, but expect to lose some bike too.
  • Cyan battery cells refill your boost. Check the radar for them.
